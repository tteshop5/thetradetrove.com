Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

We believe shopping online should feel simple, transparent and respectful of your personal information.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trove Beauty Co. handles information when you visit our website, contact us, sign up for communications, or use our online shopping services.

1. Who We Are

Trove Beauty Co. is an online beauty and self-care store operated by Trade Trove LLC.

Our website offers beauty, self-care, hair care, beauty tools and everyday beauty essentials through an online-only dropshipping business model.

For privacy-related questions, you can contact us at:

Email: contacts@thetradetrove.com

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number when provided.
  • Billing and shipping information when an order is placed.
  • Order and transaction information.
  • Messages or information you provide when contacting customer support.
  • Information submitted through newsletter or promotional signup forms.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address and pages visited.
  • Website usage information collected through cookies or similar technologies, where applicable.

We only intend to collect information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant website, customer service, marketing or transaction purpose.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating our website.
  • Processing and fulfilling orders.
  • Communicating with customers about orders or inquiries.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Responding to requests submitted through our contact forms.
  • Sending promotional communications when you have chosen to receive them.
  • Improving our website, products and customer experience.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse or unauthorized activity.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Payments and Orders

If you place an order through a connected ecommerce or payment service, payment information may be processed directly by the relevant payment provider.

Trove Beauty Co. does not intend to store complete credit card numbers or other full payment credentials on this website.

Payment providers may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Shipping and Fulfillment

For orders that are fulfilled through third-party suppliers or fulfillment partners, information necessary to fulfill and deliver an order may be shared with the appropriate service provider.

This may include information such as the customer's name, shipping address, contact information and order details.

These providers use the information to perform services such as order processing, fulfillment and delivery.

6. Service Providers and Third Parties

We may use third-party providers to help operate our website and business. Depending on the services that are active, these may include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Ecommerce and payment providers.
  • Order fulfillment and shipping providers.
  • Email and communication services.
  • Website analytics and performance services.
  • Security and fraud-prevention services.
  • Advertising or social-media services, where applicable.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We do not claim responsibility for the independent privacy practices of third-party services.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or third-party services may use cookies, local storage, pixels or similar technologies to support website functionality, security, performance, analytics or other services.

Your browser may allow you to control or block cookies. Blocking certain cookies may affect how parts of a website function.

The technologies actually used on the website may change as we add or remove services.

8. Email Communications

If you voluntarily subscribe to promotional communications, we may use your email address to send information about products, offers, updates or other Trove Beauty Co. communications.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails by using the unsubscribe option provided in the relevant message or by contacting us.

Transactional or customer-service communications may still be sent when necessary to complete or support an order or respond to a request.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining business and transaction records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud and meeting legal or regulatory obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, which can include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information.

You may also have choices concerning certain marketing communications and cookies.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your request before completing it.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

13. International Visitors

Because Trove Beauty Co. operates online and may use service providers located in different countries, information may be processed or stored outside the country where you live.

Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate safeguards for personal information handled through our service providers.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, business practices, technology, services or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
