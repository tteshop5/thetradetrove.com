Returns & Refunds

Our return and refund information is designed to keep the process clear, simple and straightforward.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Return & Refund Policy explains when a return, replacement or refund may be available for purchases made through Trove Beauty Co.

1. Our Return Approach

We want customers to have a clear and fair way to contact us when an order arrives damaged, defective, incorrect or otherwise has an issue that may qualify for a resolution.

Because products may be fulfilled by third-party suppliers, return instructions can vary depending on the product and fulfillment arrangement.

2. Contact Us Before Returning an Item

Please do not send a product back without first contacting us.

Contact us at:

contacts@thetradetrove.com

When contacting us, please include your order information and a brief description of the issue.

3. Damaged or Defective Items

Report the Problem

If an item arrives damaged or appears defective, contact us as soon as reasonably possible after delivery.

We may request photographs or other information showing the condition of the product and its packaging so that we can investigate the issue.

After reviewing the information, we may offer an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement, refund or other solution where applicable.

4. Incorrect Items

If you receive an item that is different from what you ordered, contact us as soon as possible.

Please provide your order information and photographs of the item received when requested.

We will review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution.

5. Change-of-Mind Returns

Because our products may be fulfilled through third-party suppliers, not every product may be eligible for a change-of-mind return.

Return eligibility can depend on the product, condition, fulfillment arrangement and applicable law.

Contact us before sending anything back so we can confirm whether your specific order is eligible.

Important

Do not return products to a supplier address or other address found online unless Trove Beauty Co. customer support has specifically provided return instructions for your order.

6. Product Condition

Where a return is approved, products may generally need to be returned in the condition required by the applicable return instructions.

Certain products may not be eligible for return because of hygiene, health, safety or other applicable restrictions.

7. Refunds

When a refund is approved, it will generally be issued through the original payment method or another appropriate method determined by the applicable payment provider.

The time required for a refund to appear in your account may depend on your bank, card issuer or payment provider.

A refund does not necessarily appear immediately after it has been approved.

8. Shipping Costs

Unless otherwise required by applicable law or specifically agreed by Trove Beauty Co., original shipping charges may not be refundable for change-of-mind returns.

Where an item is damaged, defective or incorrectly supplied, the appropriate shipping resolution will be determined after reviewing the circumstances.

9. Order Cancellations

If you need to request an order cancellation, contact us as soon as possible after placing the order.

We cannot guarantee cancellation once an order has entered fulfillment or has already shipped.

10. Non-Returnable Situations

Depending on the circumstances, a return or refund may not be available for:

  • Products that have been used or altered contrary to return instructions.
  • Products returned without prior authorization where authorization is required.
  • Items damaged through misuse or improper handling.
  • Requests submitted outside an applicable return period.
  • Products that are not eligible for return because of hygiene or other restrictions.
  • Situations where applicable law does not require a return or refund.

These exclusions do not limit any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

11. Refund Review

Submitting a return or refund request does not automatically guarantee approval.

Each request may be reviewed based on the order details, product condition, reason for the request, fulfillment information and applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Return & Refund Policy when our products, fulfillment arrangements, services or business practices change.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
