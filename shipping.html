Shipping Policy

Simple information about order processing, shipping options and delivery of your Trove Beauty Co. purchases.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Shipping Policy explains how orders placed through Trove Beauty Co. are processed, shipped and delivered.

1. Where We Ship

Trove Beauty Co. currently focuses on shipping products to customers within the United States.

Shipping availability may vary by product, supplier, destination or carrier service.

If a particular destination is unavailable for an item, this may be indicated during the ordering process.

2. Order Processing

Orders are normally prepared for fulfillment after payment has been successfully processed.

Processing time can vary depending on product availability and the fulfillment partner handling the order.

Typical Processing

Most orders are expected to enter fulfillment within approximately 1–3 business days.

Business days generally exclude weekends and applicable holidays. Processing may take longer during periods of unusually high order volume.

3. Shipping Options

Available shipping methods and charges are displayed during checkout when applicable.

Standard Shipping

Estimated delivery: 3–5 business days after the order has been processed and shipped.

The shipping charge may vary according to the applicable checkout settings.

Express Shipping

Estimated delivery: 1–2 business days after the order has been processed and shipped.

Express service availability may depend on the product, destination and fulfillment partner.

Delivery Estimates Are Estimates

Shipping and delivery times are estimates rather than guaranteed delivery dates unless expressly stated otherwise. Carrier delays, weather, holidays, customs procedures and other circumstances outside our reasonable control may affect delivery.

4. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges are displayed during checkout before an order is completed.

Shipping charges may depend on the selected shipping method, destination, order value, product and fulfillment arrangement.

Promotional free-shipping offers may have specific eligibility requirements.

5. Order Tracking

When tracking information is available, it may be provided through the applicable shipping or fulfillment service.

Tracking information may take some time to become active after an order has been shipped.

Carrier tracking information is provided by the carrier and may not update continuously during transit.

6. Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address when placing an order.

If you notice an incorrect address after placing an order, contact us as soon as possible at:

contacts@thetradetrove.com

We cannot guarantee that an address can be changed after an order has entered fulfillment or been shipped.

7. Delayed or Missing Packages

If a package appears to be delayed, please first check the tracking information provided by the applicable carrier.

If the package remains delayed or appears to be missing, contact us so we can review the order and available shipping information.

Carrier investigations may be required before a final resolution can be determined.

8. Damaged Packages

If your package arrives visibly damaged, please contact us as soon as reasonably possible.

Where appropriate, we may request photographs of the package, shipping label and affected product to help investigate the issue with the fulfillment or shipping provider.

9. Multiple Products in One Order

Products in the same order may sometimes be shipped separately when they are fulfilled from different locations or suppliers.

If this occurs, you may receive more than one tracking number or delivery.

10. Customs and Import Charges

For shipments where customs duties, import taxes or similar charges may apply, responsibility for those charges depends on the applicable destination, shipping arrangement and law.

Customers should review any applicable charges or requirements associated with their destination.

11. Changes to This Shipping Policy

We may update this Shipping Policy when our shipping methods, fulfillment arrangements, service providers or business practices change.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
